## Stack
- HTML5
- CSS3

## Design concept
A premium B2B textile-trading identity built around:
- dark olive + acid-lime accent
- editorial typography
- woven/fabric visual language
- product category filters
- sourcing process timeline
- Bangladesh industry benchmark section
- enquiry CTA

## Before production
Replace all placeholder company information with verified:
- UAE office address
- phone / WhatsApp
- email
- company description
- actual products
- certifications
- clients / partners
- shipping markets
- social links

The quote form is intentionally a demo. Connect it to a backend, email service, CRM, or WhatsApp workflow before launch.

The Bangladesh “Top 10” section is presented as an industry benchmark/reference set rather than an official national ranking.
